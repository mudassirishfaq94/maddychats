## Selection Criteria

### Priority Factors:
1. **Signal Protocol Experience** - Must have audited Signal or similar
2. **Post-Quantum Cryptography** - Experience with Kyber/Dilithium
3. **Messaging Application Security** - Previous messaging app audits
4. **Web/Mobile Security** - Browser and mobile platform expertise
5. **Budget Alignment** - Cost within project budget

### Red Flags to Avoid:
- No published security research
- No Signal Protocol experience
- Unrealistically low pricing
- No client references
- No formal security methodology

## Recommended Selection Process

1. **Initial Outreach** - Contact top 3-5 firms
2. **RFI (Request for Information)** - Share high-level project details
3. **Proposal Review** - Evaluate technical approach and cost
4. **Reference Checks** - Contact previous clients
5. **Final Selection** - Choose based on expertise and fit
6. **Contract Negotiation** - Define scope, timeline, deliverables
